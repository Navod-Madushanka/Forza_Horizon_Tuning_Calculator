Road & circuit tuning calculator — Japan, tarmac only
Turn 10 / Playground Games have never published the game's internal physics formulas. This version's formulas come from a personal road/touge tuning guide: springs are a % of the slider's (Max−Min) range scaled by corner weight (front softer, rear stiffer, for sharp turn-in on tight street corners and mountain passes), ARBs target a deliberately soft front (5–8) and stiff rear (40–50), damping uses Rebound at 60–70% of the max slider with Bump at 35–40% of that Rebound value, and AWD differential locks Rear Accel at 100% with a strong rear Center Bias. Front toe is set toe-OUT on purpose — that's what gives the instant turn-in this layout is built for. Treat every number as a confident starting point, then trim from in-game telemetry and feel. The low-pressure tire "meta" (~16 psi) is a deliberate contact-patch exploit, not a feel-first recommendation — it's flagged as such when enabled. Covers tarmac road and circuit racing only — no drift, drag, dirt, or cross-country logic.
